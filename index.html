Hi! My name is Yiliu Cao and I am a PhD student at Department of Statistics and Acturial Science at University of Waterloo, under the supervision of Dr. Yeying Zhu. Prevously, I earned Master of Mathematics in Statistics at University of Waterloo and Honours Bachelor of Science in Statistics and Economics at the University of Toronto.

My research focuses on causal inference, particularly the joint effects of multiple exposures and their causal interactions. My current work explores methods for directly modeling and estimating causal interaction in observational studies, with exposure dependence and model misspecification.

Outside of research, I enjoy photographying and listening to music. You can find a few of the moments I’ve captured in my photography gallery.
